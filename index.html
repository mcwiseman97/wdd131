Waxahachie, TX

Known as the "Gingerbread City," Waxahachie is celebrated for its stunning collection of historic Victorian architecture and its iconic pink granite courthouse. Located just south of Dallas, it offers a charming blend of small-town hospitality and a vibrant cultural scene, featuring local boutiques, popular annual festivals, and a growing community of artisans and professionals. It’s a place where Texas history meets modern growth, providing a scenic backdrop of tree-lined streets and historic charm.
